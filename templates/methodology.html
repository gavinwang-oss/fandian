Your front desk base wage

Tell us the hourly base wage you pay a front desk agent — the number you know off the top of your head. We add payroll tax, benefits and overhead ourselves (the loaded multiplier below), so you don’t have to work out your fully-loaded cost.

$ / hour

The formula

Time saved = for each AI-resolved message, the handling time for its type + interruption recovery, plus partial credit for messages escalated to a human.

Value = time saved × your base wage × loaded multiplier.

Message type is detected automatically from what the guest asked — you don’t enter anything. Escalated messages earn partial credit because the AI still triaged and gathered context before handing off.
